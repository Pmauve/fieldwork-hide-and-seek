Add answers to narrow the clear area. Red shading is ruled out; outlines show your questions. The starting map is Austria excluding Tirol and Vorarlberg.

Rounds are separate tabs. Changes save to the server and appear on other devices. Everyone with access sees the same workbook, including old rounds. Keep secret hider plans elsewhere.

Locations accept latitude, longitude or Google Maps dropped-pin links. Use Pick on map, check the preview, then save. GPS accuracy is shown before accepting a fix.

Matching uses your nearest place as the first candidate. Measuring uses the distance to the nearest of the supplied places. Candidate lists must follow your cards and agreed mapping app; incomplete lists are provisional. Only include eligible features inside the game map.

Same-region data includes political districts and a separate choice for Vienna's 23 municipal districts. Boundaries are simplified 2021 data; verify close border calls. Circular edges are approximated; this is a strategy tool, not metre-level adjudication.

Manual closed shapes can keep or exclude their interior, or just annotate. Lines measure distance without removing territory. Use Edit shape in an item's menu to move its vertices.

Rail mode uses a local OSM network snapshot. Stations/lines do not imply passenger service or ticket validity. Satellite and street backgrounds need mobile data. A failed server save is retained locally; export a workbook for an additional backup.

Reference: Community rule transcription. Your own rulebook takes precedence.
